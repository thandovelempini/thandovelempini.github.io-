The Break Point Problem

How much do breaks of serve and momentum really decide WTA matches, and how predictable does a player's serve become under pressure?

Approach

A serve-focused analysis of the women's tour. It looks at what happens on break points compared with ordinary points, whether winning one break changes what follows, and how serving patterns shift when a game is on the line. The results are published as an interactive Tableau dashboard.

Headline findingThe first player to break serve wins 79% of sets, across 10,827 sets. But momentum barely exists point to point: at the same score, winning the previous point doesn’t change a server’s chances of winning the next.

WNBA Rookie Projection

Which college numbers tell you whether a player will succeed in the WNBA?

Approach

A model that projects how drafted players will perform as WNBA rookies using only their college statistics. I built it in R to work outside my usual Python stack, and published the results to Tableau Public so anyone can explore the projections player by player.

Headline findingDraft position predicts who stays in the WNBA better than college stats do. But once players earn real minutes, college stats predict their production more accurately than draft slot does (error of 2.80 vs 3.06 Game Score per 40).

African Football Scouting Tool

How can player performance data help clubs find promising recruitment targets in African football?

What it does

A web app for scouts, recruitment analysts and club technical departments, starting with South Africa's Betway Premiership. Users can:

  • Search and filter by position, age, club, minutes and performance
  • Compare players in similar roles
  • Find players with similar statistical profiles
  • Build a shortlist, with a reason for each pick

Other African leagues are planned next.

Headline findingThe market for young ball-winning defensive midfielders is very thin. Across two seasons, only about three strong candidates emerged outside Mamelodi Sundowns and Orlando Pirates, which shows how quickly a targeted search narrows in the Betway Premiership.

F1 Talent Pipeline

Does success in F2 and the junior single-seater ladder predict success in Formula 1?

Approach

A Streamlit app that rates drivers on two separate scales: a Driver Performance Rating for what they've achieved given their circumstances, and a Driver Potential Rating for how much they're likely to develop. Two models answer two questions: who reaches F1, and who becomes established once there. It applies the same scouting idea as my football tool to the path into F1. Built in Python with a PostgreSQL database.

Headline findingA dominant late F2 season is a weak signal. The best junior seasons in the data mostly belong to third- or fourth-year drivers, such as de Vries (2019) and Drugovich (2022). None of them became established in F1.

About

University of Cape Town graduate

I'm a sports data analyst who builds things people can use: interactive dashboards, prediction models and scouting tools. I work across sports because each one asks different questions of the data, from a single break point in tennis to a whole season of player profiles in football.

What interests me most is scouting: using data to spot talent early. I'm especially interested in African football, where good data tools are still rare and the talent pool is huge.
