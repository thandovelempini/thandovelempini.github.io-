Football Featured project

African Football Scouting Tool

How can player performance data help clubs identify promising recruitment targets in African football?

What it does

A player scouting and profiling application designed for scouts, recruitment analysts and club technical departments, initially focused on South Africa's Betway Premiership. The tool allows users to:

  • Search and filter players by position, age, club, minutes played and performance metrics.
  • Compare players within similar positional and tactical roles.
  • Identify statistical lookalikes by finding players with similar performance profiles.
  • Build recruitment shortlists with supporting statistical reasons for each selection.

Expansion into other African leagues is planned.

Headline findingThe market for young, high-performing ball-winning defensive midfielders is surprisingly thin. Across two seasons, only a handful of strong candidates emerged outside Mamelodi Sundowns and Orlando Pirates, illustrating how quickly a targeted recruitment search can narrow the available market.

Motorsport

F1 Talent Pipeline

Does success in F2 and the junior single-seater ladder predict success in Formula 1?

A driver scouting app with separate Performance and Potential ratings, and two models: which junior drivers reach F1, and which establish themselves there.

Headline findingA dominant F2 season is not necessarily a strong signal of future F1 success. Some of the strongest junior seasons in the dataset belonged to drivers in their third or fourth F2/GP2 campaigns, including Nyck de Vries (2019) and Felipe Drugovich (2022). Neither went on to establish themselves as full-time F1 drivers.

Tennis

The Break Point Problem

How much do breaks of serve and momentum really decide WTA matches, and how predictable does a player's serve become under pressure?

A serve-focused analysis of women's Grand Slam tennis, using point-level match data and shot-charted serves.

Headline findingThe first player to break serve wins 79% of sets, across 10,827 sets. But momentum barely exists point to point. At the same score, winning the previous point does not meaningfully change a server's chances of winning the next.

Basketball

WNBA Rookie Projection

Can college statistics predict which WNBA draftees will stick in the league and how well they will perform as professionals?

Models built in R that test whether college box-score stats forecast retention into a second or third season, and professional production.

Headline findingDraft position is a stronger predictor of WNBA retention than college statistics. However, among players who earn meaningful playing time, college statistics predict production more accurately than draft position alone, achieving a lower prediction error (2.80 vs 3.06 Game Score per 40).
