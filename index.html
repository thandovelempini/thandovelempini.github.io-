What it does

A player scouting and profiling tool developed for scouts, recruitment analysts and club technical departments, with an intial focus on South Africa's Betway Premiership. The tool enables users to:

  • Search and filter players based on position, age, club, minutes played and performance metrics.
  • Compare players who perform comparable positional and tactical roles.
  • Identify statistical lookalikes by searching for players with similar comparable performance profiles.
  • Create recruitment shortlists that include statistical reasoning for each selection.

Expansion into further African leagues is planned.

Headline findingThe market for young, high-performing ball-winning defensive midfielders is very small. Over two seasons, only a few strong possibilities emerged outside Mamelodi Sundowns and Orlando Pirates, demonstrating how rapidly a targeted recruitment search can reduce the available market.

About

University of Cape Town graduate

As a sports data anlayst, my goal is to use sports data to create tools, insights and more informed decisions. I design interactive dashboards, prediction models and scouting tools. I work with a variety of sports and and the challenges they pose, from analysing a single tennis break point to creating player profiles for a whole football league.

Talent identification and scouting, which utilises data to discover promising players, assess performance and identify possibilities that could otherwise go unreported, particularly interests me. This interest extends to African sports, where I believe there is a lot of room for improved analytical tools and data-driven analysis.
